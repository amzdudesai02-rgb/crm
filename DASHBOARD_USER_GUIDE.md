# 📊 Leverage CRM Dashboard - User Guide

## Welcome to Your Command Center

The Leverage CRM Dashboard is your central hub for managing Amazon wholesale operations. This guide will walk you through all features and how to use them effectively.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Login
1. **Sign Up / Login**: Use your email or Google account to log in
2. **Onboarding Checklist**: Complete the 4-step setup guide on the dashboard:
   - ✅ Add your first brand/supplier company
   - ✅ Create a first deal in the pipeline
   - ✅ Connect Gmail and send 1 email
   - ✅ Draft a purchase order from a deal

---

## 📋 Dashboard Overview

### Top Section - Control Center
- **Personalized Greeting**: Shows your name and motivational message
- **Live Metrics**: 
  - Open Purchase Orders (POs)
  - Active Shipments in transit

### Key Metrics Cards (4 Cards)
1. **Total Deals**: Number of deals across all pipeline stages
2. **Contacts**: Total brand and supplier contacts
3. **Companies**: Active brand/supplier companies
4. **Pipeline Value**: Total expected gross revenue from all deals

---

## 🎯 Main Dashboard Sections

### 1. **Next Actions** (Left Column)
- **Purpose**: Shows your reminders and tasks
- **Features**:
  - View upcoming reminders with due dates
  - Click "Mark done" to complete tasks
  - Click "Go to pipeline →" to manage deals

**How to Use**:
- Reminders are automatically created from deals
- Click any reminder to view details
- Mark items as done when completed

---

### 2. **Getting Set Up** (Onboarding Checklist)
- **Purpose**: Guides new users through essential setup steps
- **Steps**:
  1. **Add Company**: Click to go to Companies tab
  2. **Create Deal**: Click to navigate to Pipeline
  3. **Connect Gmail**: Click to go to AI Outreach page
  4. **Draft PO**: Click to go to Pipeline and create PO from deal

**Progress Tracking**: Shows X/4 completed steps

---

### 3. **Recent Deals** (Left Column)
- **Purpose**: Quick view of your latest deals
- **Information Shown**:
  - Deal title
  - Stage (e.g., "New", "Negotiation", "Won")
  - Deal value ($)
  - Due date (if set)

**Actions**:
- Click "View all" to see full Pipeline page
- Click any deal card to view/edit details

---

### 4. **Operations Snapshot** (Right Column)
- **Purpose**: Quick view of purchase orders and shipments
- **Shows**:
  - Top 3 recent Purchase Orders
  - PO reference number
  - Status (draft, ordered, received, etc.)
  - Total amount and unit count
  - Expected arrival date (ETA)

**Actions**:
- Click "Open Operations →" to go to full Operations page
- Click any PO card to view/edit details

---

### 5. **AI Outreach Queue** (Right Column)
- **Purpose**: Shows deals that haven't been contacted yet
- **Shows**:
  - Deals without email history
  - Deal stage and owner
  - Quick access to compose emails

**Actions**:
- Click "Compose email →" to go to AI Outreach page
- Click any deal card to start email outreach

---

### 6. **Logistics Highlight** (Right Column)
- **Purpose**: Shows latest shipment status
- **Information**:
  - Shipment status (in transit, delivered, etc.)
  - Carrier name
  - Estimated arrival date (ETA)

---

## 📑 Dashboard Tabs

The dashboard has 6 main tabs at the bottom:

### 📊 **Overview Tab** (Default)
- Summary of all key metrics
- Recent deals and contacts
- Quick action buttons:
  - 📋 New Deal
  - 👤 Add Contact
  - 🏢 Add Company
  - ✉️ Send Email

---

### 📋 **Pipeline Tab**
- **Action**: Clicking this navigates to the full Pipeline page
- **Features**:
  - Kanban board view of deals
  - Drag-and-drop to move deals between stages
  - Timeline view
  - Create/edit deals
  - Convert deals to Purchase Orders

**How to Use Pipeline**:
1. Click "Pipeline" tab or "Go to pipeline →" button
2. View deals organized by stage (columns)
3. Drag deals between columns to change stages
4. Click "+" button to create new deal
5. Click any deal card to edit details
6. Use "Draft Purchase Order" in deal edit modal to create PO

---

### 👥 **Contacts Tab**
- **Purpose**: Manage all your brand and supplier contacts
- **Features**:
  - View all contacts in a grid
  - Add new contacts
  - Edit contact information

**How to Add a Contact**:
1. Click "Contacts" tab
2. Click "+ Add Contact" button
3. Fill in the form:
   - Name (required)
   - Email
   - Phone
   - Position
4. Click "Add" to save

**Contact Information Displayed**:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Position/title

---

### 🏢 **Companies Tab**
- **Purpose**: Manage brand and supplier companies
- **Features**:
  - View all companies
  - Add new companies
  - Filter by type (Brand/Supplier)

**How to Add a Company**:
1. Click "Companies" tab
2. Click "+ Add Company" button
3. Fill in the form:
   - Company Name (required)
   - Type (Brand/Supplier)
   - Country
   - Website
4. Click "Add" to save

**Company Information Displayed**:
- Company name
- Type (Brand/Supplier)
- Country location
- Website link (clickable)

---

### ✉️ **AI Outreach Tab**
- **Action**: Clicking this navigates to the AI Outreach page
- **Purpose**: Generate and send AI-powered emails to contacts

**How to Use AI Outreach**:
1. Click "AI Outreach" tab or "Compose email →" button
2. **Select a Deal** (optional): Choose a deal to auto-fill variables
3. **Select a Contact** (optional): Choose a contact to auto-fill recipient
4. **Choose Email Template**: Select from available templates
5. **Fill Variables**:
   - First name
   - Brand name
   - Category
6. **Generate Email**: Click "Generate Email" button
7. **Review & Edit**: Review the generated subject and body
8. **Connect Gmail** (if not connected): Click "Connect Gmail" button
9. **Send Email**: Click "Send via Gmail" button

**Features**:
- AI-powered email generation
- Template library
- Auto-fill from deals and contacts
- Gmail integration
- Email history tracking

---

### 📦 **Amazon Tab**
- **Purpose**: View Amazon sales data and profit metrics
- **Features**:
  - Total Revenue summary
  - Total Expenses summary
  - Net Profit calculation
  - Amazon SKU data table

**Amazon Data Table Shows**:
- SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)
- Sales amount
- Refunds amount
- Stock levels

**How to Sync Amazon Data**:
1. Click "Amazon" tab
2. If no data, click "Sync Amazon Data →" button
3. Wait for sync to complete
4. View updated metrics and SKU data

**Note**: Amazon SP-API integration requires approval from Amazon. This feature may be pending approval.

2. **Review Metrics**: Check pipeline value and deal counts
3. **Check Operations**: Review POs and shipments

2. **Update Contacts**: Add new brand/supplier contacts

- ✅ Connect Gmail early to enable email features
- ✅ Create companies before adding contacts
- ✅ Use deal-to-PO conversion to streamline operations
- ✅ Regularly update deal stages to reflect progress

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Dashboard Not Loading
- Check internet connection
- Verify you're logged in
- Refresh the page
- Check browser console for errors

### Data Not Showing
- Ensure you've created at least one item (deal, contact, company)
- Check if API is connected (look for connection error messages)
- Try refreshing the page

### Can't Send Emails
- Ensure Gmail is connected (check Profile page)
- Verify email template is selected
- Check that recipient email is valid

### Pipeline Not Updating
- Refresh the page after dragging deals
- Check browser console for API errors
- Ensure you have proper permissions
